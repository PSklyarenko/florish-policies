Data-deletion instructions

Last updated: 27 October 2026

You can request deletion of information held through Florish Marketing, Meta app ID 1069380522804844. Requests are handled by the Florish account operator.

Send your request to
pashasklyarenko0303@gmail.com
Suggested subject: Florish Marketing data deletion request

1. Identify the records you want removed

In your email, describe the connection or information involved and the deletion you are requesting. Include only the account references needed to identify the records, such as your Facebook Page name, Instagram username or relevant business/account identifier. If possible, contact the operator from an address associated with the account.

Do not send passwords, access tokens, authentication codes, payment-card details or identity documents. If ownership needs to be checked, the operator will request minimal appropriate verification information.

2. Review and deletion

The operator will review the request, locate information under the operator's control and remove or de-identify the relevant app-held data after verifying the request. This can include stored connection credentials, account-linked operational records and associated technical logs where applicable.

The operator will confirm the action taken or explain any information that must be retained for a valid legal or security purpose. The request does not authorize deletion of unrelated accounts, other people's information or published platform content.

3. Revoke the connection

You can also revoke Florish Marketing's access in your Meta account's Business Integrations or Apps and Websites settings. Find Florish Marketing and follow Meta's current removal instructions. Menu names can vary by account.

Revoking access withdraws the integration's authorization. To request removal of information previously retained in the operator's environment, send the deletion request above as well.

4. Information held by Meta or other providers

Removing app-held records does not automatically delete Facebook or Instagram posts, ads, messages, billing history or other information retained by Meta. Use the relevant platform's settings or support process for those records. Any request to remove published content through the operator should identify that content explicitly.
