Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy covers Florish Marketing, Meta app ID 1069380522804844, and its account-management and marketing-testing integration. The current setup is operated for owner-authorized Florish business assets on Facebook and Instagram.

1. Operator and contact

The integration is operated by the Florish account operator. Questions about information handled through this integration, access requests and deletion requests can be sent to pashasklyarenko0303@gmail.com.

This policy describes the Meta integration. Other platforms, separately connected provider applications and any future shopping or checkout service may have additional privacy notices.

2. Information the integration handles

Depending on the permissions granted and the operation requested, the integration may handle:

  • Account and authorization information provided by Meta, such as identifiers, names, business roles, connected assets and basic profile information made available to the app.
  • Facebook Page and Instagram professional-account information, including identifiers, profile details, posts, media, captions and publishing status.
  • Advertising information, including campaigns, ad sets, creative files, targeting settings, budgets, spend, delivery status, aggregated performance metrics, billing status and limited funding-source metadata made available by Meta to the authorized administrator.
  • Access tokens and technical authorization records needed to make permitted API requests, together with operational records and troubleshooting logs.
  • The email address, message and account references a person supplies when contacting the operator.

Processing is limited by the permissions and features the account operator authorizes. These policy pages do not provide a password, payment-card or identity-document collection form.

3. How information is used

Information is used to connect and verify authorized accounts; prepare, publish and manage approved content or advertising; read performance results; compare marketing variants; troubleshoot the integration; protect account access; and respond to requests. Operational test records may also support review and reproducibility of the operator's marketing experiments.

The current integration is an account-management tool for authorized operators. It does not independently authorize an advertiser to access another person's account.

4. Providers and disclosure

Requests and content are sent to Meta when necessary to perform an authorized Facebook or Instagram operation. Meta handles information under its own terms and privacy policy.

The current test uses locally run ByAds MCP software. The operator also uses Codex/OpenAI for assisted operation and analysis. Selected configuration, creative content, media references and redacted API results may be provided to that assistant. OpenAI's handling is described in its privacy policy and the terms applicable to the operator's account.

These public policy pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical request information, including visitor IP addresses for hosting and security, under its privacy statement. The pages themselves add no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, tracking cookies or submission forms.

Data obtained through this integration is not sold. If a further hosted marketing provider is enabled, the operator must review the information shared with that provider and update this notice or provide an additional applicable notice. Providers may process information in countries outside the account operator's country.

5. Storage and security

The current local setup keeps integration credentials in encrypted local storage and uses them in process memory while the integration runs. Operational files are held in the operator's working environment. Credentials are excluded from the public policy repository, and technical evidence is redacted to avoid exposing secret values. Access is restricted to the operator and tools authorized for the workflow.

6. Retention

Credentials are retained while the connection is needed or until they are revoked or removed. Operational records are retained as needed for account operation, troubleshooting, experiment review and relevant legal or security purposes. Following a verified deletion request, the operator removes or de-identifies associated information under the operator's control unless a valid retention obligation or security need applies; any such exception will be explained.

Content, campaigns, billing records and other information held by Meta or another provider remain subject to that provider's controls and retention rules. Removing the integration does not automatically remove material already published on a platform.

7. Your choices and requests

You can revoke the integration's permissions in the relevant Meta account settings. You can also contact the operator to request information about app-held data, correction or deletion, or other applicable privacy rights. The operator may request minimal information to verify the requester and locate the relevant records.

Follow the data-deletion instructions to request removal. Do not email passwords, access tokens, payment-card details or identity documents.

8. Changes

Changes to the integration's data handling will be reflected on this page by updating the policy and its effective date. For questions, contact pashasklyarenko0303@gmail.com.
