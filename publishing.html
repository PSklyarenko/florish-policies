Florish Publishing Lab

An experimental, locally hosted publishing setup operated for Florish’s own authorized social accounts. This is not a public sign-up or shopping service.

The operator is testing self-hosted Postiz and TryPost: preparing posts from supplied media, scheduling authorized publication, and reading available performance data. Codex assists with operation, logging and comparison. TikTok access is limited by the app’s actual permissions, environment and review status.

Sandbox and private-upload checks do not establish public posting access. Public TikTok publishing is not currently enabled in this setup. The operator does not claim that the app has passed TikTok’s Direct Post audit.

These pages provide information only. Authentication and publishing occur in the operator’s local application and TikTok’s official authorization screens. Do not send passwords, access tokens, payment information or identity documents to this website.
