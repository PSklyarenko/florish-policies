Publishing privacy notice

Effective and last updated: 28 October 2026

This notice covers Florish Publishing Lab, including the owner-operated Postiz and TryPost test installations and their separately authorized TikTok connections. The existing Florish Marketing privacy policy describes the Meta integration.

Operator and purpose

The Florish account operator runs this experimental integration to manage authorized content and review marketing-test results. It is currently limited to the operator’s own accounts. Contact: pashasklyarenko0303@gmail.com.

Information handled

If an account is connected and an operation is authorized, the local software may process account identifiers and permitted profile details; OAuth access/refresh tokens and granted scopes; creative media, captions, draft/schedule settings, publishing identifiers and status; and available aggregate views, likes, comments and shares. Only information made available by the platform and the granted permissions can be accessed. Contact messages may contain an email address and account references supplied by the sender.

Use and disclosure

Information is used to authenticate the operator’s accounts, prepare and publish authorized content, retrieve available results, compare variants, troubleshoot failures and protect access. Requests and approved content are sent directly to TikTok when required for those operations. TikTok processes data under its own terms and privacy policy. Postiz and TryPost servers run locally; no commercial hosted marketing backend is enabled for these tests.

The operator uses Codex/OpenAI for assisted operation. Selected creative content, configuration descriptions, media references and redacted results may be processed by that assistant under the operator’s applicable OpenAI terms and OpenAI’s privacy policy. Account passwords, identity documents and raw secrets are not intentionally placed in the public evidence or policy repository.

GitHub Pages hosts these notices and the approved public test clip. GitHub may process technical hosting-request information under its privacy statement. These static pages add no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, tracking cookies or submission forms. Content deliberately published on a social network, and media deliberately hosted at a public URL, can be viewed by others under that service’s rules.

Storage and retention

Local application data and media are stored in the operator’s Docker volumes and working environment. Application configuration, local API credentials and keys are kept in access-restricted local files outside the public repository; connected-account tokens are handled by the local software and its database. Configuration files are not represented as encrypted merely because filesystem access is restricted. Technical evidence is redacted to avoid exposing raw secrets.

Connections are retained while needed or until revoked or removed. Operational records are retained for the operator’s test review, troubleshooting and relevant security or legal needs. A verified deletion request is handled as described in the publishing data-deletion instructions. Platform-held posts and records remain subject to TikTok’s controls and retention. Removing this connection does not automatically remove an already published post.

Choices and requests

The account owner may revoke the connection through TikTok’s app-permission controls and request removal of locally held connection data. Information obtained through these integrations is not sold. Send access, correction or deletion requests to pashasklyarenko0303@gmail.com; do not include passwords, tokens, payment details or identity-document scans. The operator may request minimal information to verify ownership and locate records.

Changes

This notice will be updated when the setup’s actual handling changes. No public publishing approval, legal certification or platform endorsement is claimed.
