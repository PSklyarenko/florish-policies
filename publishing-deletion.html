Publishing data deletion

Last updated: 28 October 2026

These instructions cover locally held data for Florish Publishing Lab’s Postiz, TryPost and TikTok test connections. For the Meta app, use the separate Meta data-deletion instructions.

Stop future access

Revoke the integration through TikTok’s app-permission controls. The operator can also disconnect the relevant account in the local publishing application and stop its future jobs. Revoking access does not itself remove content already published on TikTok.

Request local deletion

Email pashasklyarenko0303@gmail.com with the subject “Florish Publishing Lab data deletion,” the connected username and which connection or local records you want removed. Do not send a password, OAuth token, payment details or identity-document scan. The operator may request minimal proof of account ownership before deleting data.

After verifying and locating the request, the operator removes or de-identifies the requested local connection/token records and associated app-held data under their control, unless retention is needed for a valid legal or security reason. The operator will explain any relevant exception and confirm what was removed. Copies held by TikTok, GitHub or another provider remain governed by that provider’s controls and retention.

Public content and evidence

Ask separately if you want the operator to remove a run-owned public test post or publicly hosted creative. Already shared copies may remain outside the operator’s control. Redacted technical evidence may be retained where it no longer identifies the account or where a relevant retention reason applies.
